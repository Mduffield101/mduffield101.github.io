A useful nudge.

Fig Assistant sends its owner email when a task needs attention, then brings their reply back to the relevant assistant conversation.

What Fig does

Fig sends action requests, approval requests, deadline reminders, failure alerts and links to work ready for review. It runs as a private tool for its owner, with no public sign-up.

Why it connects to Gmail

Gmail send access lets Fig deliver notifications from its dedicated mailbox to one approved recipient. Read-only access lets it find replies from that recipient and route their text to the matching Codex conversation.

Gmail authorisation is granted by the mailbox owner through Google. Access can be revoked in their Google Account settings.

Data and privacy

This website explains the tool. It does not display emails, task conversations or private documents.
