Privacy policy.

Fig Assistant is a personal automation tool operated by its owner. This policy describes the dedicated Gmail integration and this public information website.

Information Fig accesses

Fig requests Gmail read-only and send permissions for its dedicated mailbox. Read-only permission technically permits reading mailbox messages; the reply watcher searches for recent messages from one approved sender and checks the sender again before routing a reply.

It processes message identifiers, sender and recipient addresses, subjects, message text and conversation identifiers. Outgoing notifications may contain task summaries, links and approved attachments.

How information is used

Information is used to send notifications, recognise replies, avoid duplicate processing and resume the matching assistant conversation. The watcher removes quoted email history where possible and applies content checks before routing a reply.

Reply text and its email subject are passed to OpenAI’s Codex to continue the relevant task. The conversation is handled under the owner’s applicable OpenAI account terms and data controls. Google processes mailbox data to provide Gmail.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored encrypted on the owner’s computer. Local records retain delivery status, subjects, timestamps, message and task identifiers, and content hashes. These records do not intentionally store complete email bodies, although diagnostic errors may contain snippets.

Messages remain in Gmail, and routed replies become part of the relevant Codex conversation. Local records and service-held data remain until removed by the owner or under the relevant service’s retention settings; Fig does not currently impose an automatic deletion schedule.

Sharing and permitted use

Fig does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Data is passed only as needed to operate the notification and reply workflow described above. Fig’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Control and deletion

The mailbox owner can revoke Fig’s Google access at Google Account connections. Revocation stops future API access; it does not automatically delete existing emails, local records or Codex conversations. The owner can remove those separately in the relevant service or local storage.

Privacy questions or deletion requests can be sent to messagesfromfig@gmail.com.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. They contain no analytics scripts, tracking cookies, forms or public sign-in. Fonts are served with the site. GitHub may process technical connection information to operate its hosting service, as described in the GitHub privacy statement.

Changes

This page will be updated when the described data practices change. The revision date appears above.
